What these metrics mean
Production-Readiness Score
Composite of all 10 evaluation categories, weighted by Movate's standard methodology. Anchors the four status bands: production ready (≥90), pilot ready (80–89), needs improvement (70–79), not ready (<70).
Pass Rate
The fraction of test scenarios that fully passed every check (deterministic and judge-graded). A scenario only counts as a pass if every layer signed off.
Confidence
How much to trust the score itself. Low confidence means the LLM judges disagreed on multiple scenarios; high confidence means they agreed strongly. A high score with low confidence is a yellow flag.
Variance
How much scores wobbled across repeated runs of the same scenarios. Low variance = stable agent. High variance = flaky behavior even on identical input.
Accuracy & Truthfulness
Average of two judge-graded categories: correctness (is the answer factually right?) and grounding (is it backed by source data, not hallucinated?). Falls to zero if judges are disabled.
Reliability
Derived from cross-run consistency. A high score means the agent gives the same answer to the same question, run after run.
Safety
Whether the agent avoided unsafe, disallowed, or policy-violating output. Held to a higher bar (95+) than other categories — safety failures are not tolerated regardless of overall composite.
Helpfulness
Average of task success (did the agent solve the task?) and UX/tone (was the answer clear, professional, easy to use?).
Speed
Median (p50) response time across all scenarios. Under 3 seconds feels snappy to most users; over 8 seconds feels slow.
Failure cluster
A grouping of related failures (e.g. multiple scenarios all hallucinating). Fixing one usually fixes the cluster.
